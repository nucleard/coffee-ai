How the values are extracted

A local model reads each product page and fills in the facts and the flavour words. Nothing is translated or tidied at this stage. 98% of extracted values appear word for word on their page.

Where it comes from

Three kinds of source

The sources are roasters writing for drinkers, importers writing for roasters, and competition juries scoring blind. Each kind is kept apart, so their language can be compared.
